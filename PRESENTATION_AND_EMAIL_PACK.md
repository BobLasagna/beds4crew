# Presentation, Email, and Video Pack

## A) 10-Slide Investor Deck (Simple + High Signal)

### Slide 1 — Vision
- Build the operating system for modern property/crew rentals.
- Convert fragmented workflows into one platform.

### Slide 2 — Market Problem
- Hosts juggle listings, bookings, communication, and payments across tools.
- Fragmentation causes churn, slow response times, and lost revenue.

### Slide 3 — Product Solution
- Listing + discovery + booking + support + billing in one product.
- Role-aware flows for guests, hosts, and admins.

### Slide 4 — Revenue Engine
- Stripe subscription tiers unlock host capabilities.
- Webhook-backed subscription state keeps access and billing in sync.

### Slide 5 — Trust & Security
- httpOnly cookie sessions + CSRF protection.
- Admin allowlists and production environment controls.

### Slide 6 — Reliability Operations
- Deployment runbooks (Render + MongoDB Atlas).
- Troubleshooting guides for Stripe webhooks and email delivery.

### Slide 7 — Performance & UX
- Lazy loading, caching, compression, lean DB queries.
- Consistent mobile-first UI system and reusable components.

### Slide 8 — Customer Experience
- Booking lifecycle notifications.
- Support center + data-driven support chat flows.

### Slide 9 — Current Status
- Production-ready architecture and checklists in place.
- Clear path from pilot users to paid subscriptions.

### Slide 10 — Ask
- Investor: funding for go-to-market and growth execution.
- Client: pilot onboarding with measurable operational outcomes.
